System dynamics for supply chain networks

Supply chains fail as systems, not as incidents.

A late shipment, a supplier default, a demand spike — each looks like a discrete event. It rarely is. Estay Dynamics models the feedback, delay, and structure that turn a local disruption into a network-wide one, and designs networks that hold up when it happens.
