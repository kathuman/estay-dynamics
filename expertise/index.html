Areas of expertise

Four lenses, one underlying question.

Every engagement — modeling, network design, or a facilitated workshop — comes back to the same question: what structure produces this outcome? These are the four formal toolkits I use to answer it, each with its own entry point below.

01 / Feedback & structure

System Dynamics

Causal loop diagrams, stock-flow simulation, and group model building — mapping the feedback and delay that turn a local disruption into a network-wide one, and building the diagnosis together with the team that has to live with it.

Read more → 02 / Propagation, not probability

Systemic Risk Analysis

Why a risk's real cost is rarely the initiating event — it's whether the network's own structure amplifies it. Quantifying propagation pathways instead of freezing a likelihood × impact score.

Read more → 03 / The value of waiting

Real Options

Pricing the right — not the obligation — to defer, stage, expand, or abandon a network decision under genuine uncertainty, instead of a single locked-in NPV that assumes the future is known.

Network Design Optimization

Mixed-integer and stochastic programming for facility, sourcing, and capacity decisions, with Monte Carlo sampling to characterize the full distribution of outcomes a design should expect — not just its best case.
