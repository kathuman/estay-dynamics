Areas of expertise

Seven areas of expertise, each tied to a business decision.

Every engagement starts from a business situation — a problem that keeps returning, a risk that cannot yet be quantified, an investment under real uncertainty, an operational or engineering change that is expensive to get wrong — not from a modelling technique. Each page sets out the situations where the approach is needed, what it delivers and how an engagement runs.
