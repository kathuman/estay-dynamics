The services

01 · System dynamics

Dynamic Risk & Resilience Modeling

Static risk registers score a threat once and freeze it. We model how a disruption compounds through delay, capacity limits and the responses around it, so you can stress-test your network and act on early warning.

How it works → 02 · MILP / stochastic programming

Network Design Optimization

Facility, sourcing and capacity decisions usually get optimized on landed cost alone. We put a number on flexibility — deferral, expansion, dual sourcing, switching — so the trade-off against cost is explicit.

How it works → 03 · Causal mapping / simulation

Systems Diagnostics & Executive Workshops

Teams often diagnose structural problems as behavioural ones. Facilitated causal mapping, backed by simulation, shows your leadership team which fixes are durable — and which only move the symptom.
