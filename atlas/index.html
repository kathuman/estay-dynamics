Purpose & use

A working model of a network under disruption — real ports, corridors, and disruption signals, with named response scenarios (reroute, buffer, alternate-source) you can switch between to see how the network's shape changes.

Layers toggle what's shown. Scenario overlay swaps in a response and reports its cost (extra transit days, suspended lanes). Your network lets you import your own CSV instead of the sample data — nothing leaves your browser.
