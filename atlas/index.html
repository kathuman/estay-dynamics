Purpose & use

A working model of a network under disruption — real ports, corridors, and disruption signals, with named response scenarios (reroute, buffer, alternate-source) you can switch between to see how the network's shape changes.

Layers toggle what's shown. Scenario overlay swaps in a response and reports its cost (extra transit days, suspended lanes). Your network lets you import your own CSV instead of the sample data — nothing leaves your browser.

Your network

Import your own ports, warehouses, and routes as CSV. Nothing leaves your browser — files are parsed locally.

Your routes are exposed to disruptions and scenarios the same way the sample network is: route through an existing port/DC id (e.g. rotterdam, la-lb, panama) to inherit that site's risk, or name your route's lane to match one (e.g. include “Suez” for the Red Sea crisis). Pick “Both” below so the sample ports your routes reference still render.

Scenario overlay

Activate one or more responses at once — real disruptions often overlap (the Red Sea crisis and the Panama drought, for instance, have coincided).
